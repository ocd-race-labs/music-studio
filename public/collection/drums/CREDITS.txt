Music Studio Colección: rock drum kits

Each kit was built from one of these free libraries. Hits were chosen and edited, microphones mixed, and every kit
tuned, equalized, compressed and placed in rooms for its style. Kit names describe a style and an era only.

- SM Drums by Scott McLean, Tod Stillwell and Suleiman Ali. Public domain / free use.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SMDrums
  Kits: Estudio 60, Arena 80 compuerta, Pop punk 90; china in Garage revival and Punk 77.
- Big Rusty Drums by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums
  Kits: Escalera de piedra, Grunge 90.
- MuldjordKit by Lars Muldjord / DrumGizmo. CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.MuldjordKit
  Kits: Alternativo moderno, Thrash 80, Metal moderno.
- DRSKit by DrumGizmo (Lars Muldjord, Bent Bisballe Nyeng) and Jes Eiler / DRSDrums. CC BY 4.0.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.DRSKit
  Kits: Garage revival, Punk 77.
