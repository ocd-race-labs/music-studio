Music Studio Colección: electric guitar and bass

Both are recorded direct (no amp), edited (onset, length, fade) and played through the app's modelled gear
(preamp, amp, cabinet, compressor, tape, effects) to make each tone. The open low E of the bass was retuned 20 cents down.
