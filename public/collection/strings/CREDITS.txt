Music Studio Colección: electric guitar and bass

Both are recorded direct (no amp), edited (onset, length, fade) and played through the app's modelled gear
(preamp, amp, cabinet, compressor, tape, effects) to make each tone. The open low E of the bass was retuned 20 cents down.

- Emilyguitar by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.emilyguitar
- Growlybass by Karoryfer Samples. CC0 1.0. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.growlybass

Music Studio Colección: orchestra, piano, vibes, brass, woodwinds and upright bass

Real recordings, one note every few semitones in two dynamics (mf, f). Each note is pitch-checked, trimmed,
given a longer sustain by crossfading its steady middle, faded and levelled (tools/collection/orchestra.py).

- VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios. CC0 1.0. https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
  (violin, violin and cello sections, cello, upright bass pizzicato, flute, trumpet, muted trumpet, horns)
- Versilian Community Sample Library by Versilian Studios. CC0 1.0. https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  (Steinway B piano, tenor saxophone, vibraphone)
